Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions
Answered!

How do I start a project with Innovit Labs?

It starts with a Discovery Call. We discuss your business goals, technical requirements, and budget. From there, we provide a technical roadmap and a cost estimate before writing any code.

Why is Innovit Labs different from other dev agencies?

We are Business-First Engineers. We don't just write code; we build assets. We specialize in modern tech (AI, Automation, SaaS) and prioritize scalability, ensuring your software grows with your revenue.

How long does a typical project take?

An MVP (Minimum Viable Product) usually takes 6-10 weeks. Larger enterprise platforms can take 3-6 months. We work in 2-week sprints so you see progress continuously.

Is Innovit Labs startup-friendly?

Yes. We work with founders to cut unnecessary features and focus on the "Core Value Proposition" to get you to market quickly and affordably.

Do you sign an NDA?

Absolutely. We respect your Intellectual Property. We sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) before we even hear your specific idea to ensure your concept remains yours.

How do we communicate during the project?

We set up a dedicated Slack/WhatsApp group for daily updates and use Trello/Jira for task tracking. We also have weekly video calls to review progress and plan the next sprint.
