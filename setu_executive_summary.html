Problem

  • Affordability and continuity of care remain critical unmet needs
Solution
  • One platform connecting users to tests, doctors, medicine, and government schemes
  • Home & camp-based diagnostics via a dedicated Lab Technician App
  • Unified digital health record with ABHA integration for every citizen
  • End-to-end care continuity: Test → Report → Doctor → Medicine → Follow-up
